Frequently Asked Questions

How often do students fill out the survey?

▼

Once per week. The survey is intentionally short so completion stays high, and professors can track trends across the term.

Can professors adjust groups manually?

▼

Yes. GroupPilot provides recommendations, but instructors always stay in control and can edit teams before publishing.

How does meeting setup work?

▼

Students submit availability windows, GroupPilot finds overlaps by team, and professors can send meeting times in a few clicks.
